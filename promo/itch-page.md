# 2003

## Short description

Two gates, four tracks. Catch each orbiting note as it crosses its gate.

## About

Follow the orbiting note and hit its matching gate as it crosses. Play through four tracks, from Super to Extreme, and try to make it to the end of every song. Play in your browser, or download the Windows or Linux version.

## Controls

Use A/D or F/J, left/right mouse buttons, either half of the touchscreen, or the left/right gamepad bumpers to hit the gates.
