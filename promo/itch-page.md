# 2003

## Short description

Catch each note at the coral or blue gate. Can you keep the orbit going for a whole song?

## About

A coral note sweeps around the ring. Watch it approach the left gate, then tap as it crosses. Blue notes belong on the right. On Super the two sides alternate, giving you a chance to find your timing to DJ Okawari's *Flower Dance*.

Hyper, Ultra and Extreme pick up the pace and change how the notes travel. A tight orbit gives you less time to react. Let a note slip past and the run ends, with your best percentage saved for the next attempt. Reach 100% to clear the track.

Play in your browser or download the game for Windows or Linux.

## Controls

Use A/D or F/J, left/right mouse buttons, either half of the touchscreen, or the left/right gamepad bumpers to hit the gates.
